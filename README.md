## ⚖️ Why DMCA-Ignored Hosting Matters
 
> **DMCA** = Digital Millennium Copyright Act (US, 1998). Any copyright holder can send a takedown notice, and a **US-based host must comply immediately**, no questions asked.
 
**Why this is a constant risk for manga/anime sites:**
 
- 🇯🇵 Shueisha, Kodansha, and Viz Media actively send DMCA notices worldwide
- 🤖 Automated DMCA bots fire thousands of takedown requests daily, often against legitimate fan sites
- ⏱️ One complaint to a US host can take your entire site down within 24 hours
**If you're on a US-based host** (GoDaddy, Bluehost, HostGator, SiteGround, Cloudflare, etc.), they must comply with every notice. Even legal content goes offline first, you then file a counter-notice and wait **10–14 business days** to come back, losing traffic, revenue, and rankings the whole time.
